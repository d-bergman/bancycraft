# Dune map source and release review

BancyCraft's local Dune atlas is generated from the Dune: Awakening Community Wiki's `Map:Hagga Basin`, `Map:Deep Desert`, and `Map:Overland` pages. The marker data file records the source revision of each page. The map images are the corresponding wiki file uploads. The wiki states that its content is [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) unless otherwise noted. The map UI links back to the wiki and attributes Funcom game artwork.

This is a free fan project, not an official Funcom map. [Funcom's fan-use FAQ](https://www.funcom.com/legal/) welcomes free noncommercial fan use of released game material, subject to its stated conditions and revocation. The app is free to download and use; its optional Donate link does not gate the map or any other feature. The bundled images are from the three cited Community Wiki map pages, and the app displays wiki/license credit and a Funcom copyright notice alongside the map. The wiki's CC BY-NC-SA notice says "unless otherwise noted"; individual image pages do not provide a separate license statement. Treat image rights as subject to the wiki and Funcom terms and remove them if a rights holder requests it. Do not use third-party map tiles or datasets from gaming.tools or TH.GL in this atlas without their permission.

The current wiki revisions provide 642 Hagga Basin, 31 Deep Desert, and 23 Overland markers. This is a snapshot, not a claim that every location or weekly drop has been mapped. The item-to-pin lookup matches 275 of the 329 unique items to at least one pin. The other 54 items remain searchable through 20 written source references, clearly marked as lacking a coordinate. Regenerate the index with `python scripts/catalog/dune-map.py` after checking a wiki revision, then review counts, duplicate names, changed positions, and source attribution before replacing the bundled data.
